2. Paste the **Title** and **Description** from above.
3. **Thumbnail:** upload `thumbnail_1280x720.jpg`. Custom thumbnails need a phone-verified channel.
4. **Audience:** No, it's not made for kids.
5. **Show more:**
   - **Altered content:** Yes, because the vocals are AI-generated (see below).
   - **Tags:** paste the list above.
   - **Category:** Music.
   - **Language:** English.
6. **Visibility:** upload as Unlisted first. Watch it once on your phone, check the chapters appear, then switch to Public.
7. **Short:** upload `previews/TikTok_Famous_hook_9x16_share.mp4` from the YouTube app as a Short. Title: `47 likes → 1 million 📱 #TikTokFamous #Shorts`. Use the Short's "related video" field to link the full video.
